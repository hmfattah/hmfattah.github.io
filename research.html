Research

My research sits where AI meets computing education. I build environments where students learn AI by running experiments themselves, and I study how AI tools are changing what students need to learn.

I also study how large language models reason, and fail to reason, about ethics in high-stakes decisions.

Research Interests

  • Hands-on, reproducible AI learning
  • AI-assisted programming

Featured project · 2025 – Present

AI Sandbox: An Interactive Environment for Reproducible AI Learning

The AI Sandbox is where my teaching and research meet. Instead of only reading about models, students run experiments, compare models, and see the results for themselves.

  • Developing a modular, policy-aware sandbox for reproducible experiments, model benchmarking, and interactive visualization
  • Designing hands-on activities with synthetic data, automated assessments, and guided model comparisons
  • Running a Fall 2026 classroom pilot to evaluate student learning and engagement
